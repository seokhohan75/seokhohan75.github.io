A front-end interface redesign that brings a consistent structure to building overviews, floor plans, and equipment screens.

01 / The approach

Make the system
easier to read.

My work at Therma focused on establishing the overall interface look and keeping it consistent throughout the platform. I collaborated with project managers, programmers, and designers to connect visual standards with implementation needs.

The design framework uses a shared navigation bar, consistent equipment imagery, and structured information groups across different screen types.

05 / Design decisions

Consistency in the details.

A shared navigation system

A persistent top navigation establishes a recognizable structure across building, floor, and equipment views.

Information in context

Spatial graphics connect equipment information with its location, while grouped summaries support a building-wide view.

Reusable visual patterns

Consistent headings, symbols, and status treatments provide a foundation for extending the interface across projects.

06 / Contribution

More than a
visual refresh.

I created the platform’s visual design direction and reusable templates, coordinated with technical teams, and mentored designers on consistent production practices.

The selected screens demonstrate a visual framework designed to carry across building, floor, and equipment views.
