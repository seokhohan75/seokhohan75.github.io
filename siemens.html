01 / Context & contribution

From the building
to the equipment.

I designed BAS front-end graphics in Siemens Desigo CC, working with project managers, engineers, and specialists to translate project requirements into a coordinated visual interface.

My work included floor-plan views, equipment illustrations, system diagrams, and summary screens, alongside on-site implementation support.

The examples below show how a shared navigation and graphic language connects different levels of building information.

05 / Visual approach

One language.
Multiple scales.

Spatial context

Floor layouts place zones and equipment within the building, connecting technical information with physical location.

Equipment detail

Cutaway illustrations show component relationships, with readings and controls arranged around the equipment.

Consistent structure

Repeated navigation, headers, and grouped panels carry a recognizable structure from summaries to detailed system views.
